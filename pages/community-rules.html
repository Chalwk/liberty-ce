🛡️ community rules

Liberty Gaming is all about good times and great Halo CE matches. Our rules are simple - if you're caught breaking them, you may be kicked or muted. We really don't like banning anyone, but we will if someone continually fails to control themselves. Play smart, respect others, and you'll fit right in.

Respect others

  • No offensive or inappropriate nametags - keep it clean.
  • Zero tolerance for insults, harassment, or racist remarks.
  • Treat everyone like a fellow Spartan - we're here to have fun.

Fair play & gameplay

  • Don't cheat. No aimbots, wallhacks, or any form of unfair advantage.
  • No intentional teamkilling, teamshooting, or damaging your own team's vehicles.
  • Avoid unnecessary team switching - it unbalances the game.
  • Don't constantly block portals or intentionally disrupt the flow of play.
  • Excessive chatting during a match (instead of playing) is disruptive - keep it brief.

Chat & communication

  • No spamming text or voice (Discord) chat.
  • Refrain from disruptive, offensive, or overly loud voice chat.
  • If you have a problem with someone, contact an admin - don't start a flame war.

Exploits & glitches

  • Don't abuse map glitches, geometry holes, or game bugs to gain an unfair advantage.
  • Report any major exploits to the admin team so we can fix or address them.

Listen to admins

  • Admins are volunteers who keep the servers fun and fair.
  • Respect their decisions - if you disagree, discuss it politely after the match.
  • Repeatedly ignoring admin instructions will lead to mutes or kicks.
As Perico puts it: “Cheating is only for idiots with very small dicks and obviously no real skills.” So, seriously - just don't cheat. Be better than that.

How we enforce

First offence - usually a warning (verbal or via chat).

Repeated / serious offence - temporary mute or kick from server.

Continual issues or major violations - temporary ban (duration depends on severity).

Permanent ban - only as a last resort for extreme or repeated behaviour.

These rules apply in-game and on our Discord. If you see someone breaking them, ping an admin or send a direct message.

We're here to enjoy Halo CE together - thanks for being part of Liberty.
