Welcome to Liberty Gaming

Liberty is a Halo Custom Edition gaming and social community founded in 2024 by former LTD2 clan members. We host Halo CE servers featuring both classic and custom maps across popular game modes such as CTF, Slayer, and Oddball, along with a dedicated Race server. Join us for epic battles, custom content, and a friendly community of Halo players.
